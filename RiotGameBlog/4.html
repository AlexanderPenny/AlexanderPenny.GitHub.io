Development Diary 4 - No Longer Continuing

Date: 22/11/2026 (DD/MM/YYYY)

Update

After talking it over, the team and I have all decided not to continue working on this game. There are a few reasons for this, one way or another, but mainly it comes down to a lack of time that any of us could put towards it.

I would be open to picking it back up, providing a team could be put together to pursue it. If that is something you would be interested in, get in contact with me.

Thank you to everyone who put work into this project.
